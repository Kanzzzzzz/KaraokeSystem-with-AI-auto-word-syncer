Pick a song, press SPACE to play it, and tap J as each line - or, in Words mode, each word - begins. The editor records the exact moment and writes a proper .lrc file when you save.

How to do this well
Listen through once first so you know the song.
Tap the moment a line starts, not when you read it.
Click any line to jump there and re-time from that point.
← → nudge the selected word 0.05s (Shift 0.01s)
↑ ↓ select the previous / next word
K mark where the line's singing stops
R re-tap the current line
How to do this well
Slow down with S - 75% makes fast lines far easier.
Press K after a line's last word when a break follows, so its wipe stops with the singing.
Click a word to hear it and select it; J then re-taps from there.
Taps are shifted 0.15s earlier to allow for reaction time.
